# Hi, I'm Eddie Arellano

I'm a software engineer with a long-term ambition to establish an independent research clinic dedicated to advancing our understanding and treatment of autism.

My interest in this field began with my younger brother, who is nonspeaking and autistic. His experiences motivated me to pursue software engineering as a means of developing the technical skills, financial independence, and resources to eventually contribute to scientific research.

Today, I work as a software engineer at **CPMC**, building financial forecasting, analytics, and internal software systems.

Outside of work, I'm developing **Neraptic**, an augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) platform intended to help nonspeaking individuals communicate more independently.

My broader interests span neuroscience, psychology, computation, and the development of technologies that can meaningfully improve human lives.
